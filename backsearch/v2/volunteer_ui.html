Pause freezes the worker processes (SIGSTOP). They keep their memory (up to several GB per worker), so pause when you need the CPU, stop when you need the RAM. Stop lets each worker print its remaining subtrees, reports them, and exits the client; nothing is lost. Reducing the worker count lets surplus workers finish their current job first.
