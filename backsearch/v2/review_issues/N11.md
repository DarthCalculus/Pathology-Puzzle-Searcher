## Summary
Option (b), enforcing K<=4, is the simplest and is exact on every grid. A committed walk at token k means the player steps onto a committed cell other than the exit (which is never re-entered) and other than its previous cell. The previous cell is forbidden by the anti-wiggle rule after a walk, and holds the pushed block after a push. Any other committed cell closes a grid cycle of length >= 4 with the player's path, or of length >= 6 through a push's collinear B-P-C cells. So k >= 5. At K<=4 the listing tree (bulk off) and the replay tree (bulk on) are identical, and no root ends in a committed-walk run. All 2,761 campaign #1 layer-4 roots contain no committed walk. Option (a), listing with bulk semantics, is also exact for any K, by the same dedup-order argument that split exactness uses, but needs three code changes. Option (c) is complete only with a new seed syntax.

## Verifier (CONFIRMED, critical, refuted=False)
**The defect is real.**
- `run_estimate` (backsearch.c:4060-4137) builds the `--list-layer` layer with `g_bulk_walk_active` still 0. That flag is set only in `run_exit_search` at 4371, and main calls only `run_estimate` when estimating (5769). So the listing tree is the bulk-off tree: committed walks are single steps.
- Jobs replay the listed seeds with bulk on:
  - backsearch.c:2060-2061 folds a trailing committed-walk run into one bulk edge (`bulk_walk=1`, `wh=0`).
  - expand at 2476 then sets `skip_committed_walks=1` for that root.
- In the bulk-on tree, the further committed-walk continuations of a root R are bulk siblings of R, not its children. They sit at depth > K, so they are not listed, and the replay of R skips them. No job ever explores those subtrees.
- The lead's run confirms the loss: dyn/roots_l8.log shows 5x5 exit 7, at most 3 blocks, 19,680 layer-8 roots, "lost 19271 (depth>=8: 9566)". The deepest lost levels are 50 moves. roots_l8_nobulk.log shows "(depth>=8: 0)".

**Why K<=4 is exact.** I checked the geometric argument independently.
- The committed cells are the exit, every player cell and every B cell of variants 3/4. Variant-2 B cells are already block cells, and block cells are always former player cells.
- The player never re-enters the exit (2518, 1935).
- The previous cell is either blocked, after a push, or forbidden by `wh_forbidden`, after a walk.
- Any other committed target closes an even closed walk of length at least 4 through player cells. Through a variant-3/4 B cell, which is collinear B-P-C with the block parked on the middle cell, the closed walk is at least 6 long.
- So the first committed walk can only be token 5 or later.
- `bulk_generate` also yields nothing at depth <=3. The committed component there is only the current walk segment, since cells behind the anchor are blocked. For segment cell p_j, adist <= j-a < (d-a)+(d-j), so the walk-seg prune at ~2349 removes every candidate before any dedup insert.
- Hence the bulk-off and bulk-on trees coincide through depth 4. The replayed depth-4 root gets the same wh, seg and adjflags as the monolithic node, and its own `bulk_generate` runs in both.
- The `P==exit` adjflags reset in expand has no counterpart in `apply_seed_step`. That reset is dead code, because the player stands on the exit only at depth 0, when there are no blocks.
