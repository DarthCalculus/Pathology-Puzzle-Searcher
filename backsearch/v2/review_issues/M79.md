# M79: The completed-campaign credit list silently drops everyone under 1 CPU-hour

verdict CONFIRMED, severity low, category bug
locations: campaigns.js:49 (card() filters contributors with cpu_h >= 1); collective.html ('credited to everyone who took part')

## Summary
card() keeps only contributors with cpu_h >= 1 when building the table, but collective.html promises the result is 'credited to everyone who took part'. Volunteers who contributed under an hour are silently left out.

## Source report F113 (server-web)
**The completed-campaign credit list silently drops everyone under 1 CPU-hour** at /Users/george/PathologyRecords/server/public/campaigns.js:49

*summary*: card() filters contributors with cpu_h >= 1 before building the table, but collective.html promises the result is 'credited to everyone who took part'.

*failure_scenario*: Campaign #1: 'anonymous' finished 44 jobs (0.15 CPU-h) and is missing from the campaigns.html table. Nothing indicates that anyone was omitted.

*evidence*: campaigns.js:49 `const main = c.contributors.filter(x => x.cpu_h >= 1);`
collective.html:86 `credited to everyone who took part.`
snapshot contributors: `anonymous|44|4|40|0.15|...`

*suggested_fix*: List everyone, or add a line such as '+N others with less than 1 CPU-hour'.
